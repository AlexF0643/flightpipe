# Decision log

Record every judgment call: thresholds, schema choices, things tried and dropped. Newest first.

## Template

### YYYY-MM-DD: short title
- **Context:** what problem or question came up
- **Options:** what you considered
- **Decision:** what you chose
- **Why:** evidence (numbers, plots) behind it
- **Revisit if:** what would change your mind
